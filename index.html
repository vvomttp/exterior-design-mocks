Exterior to Interior UX Prototypes

Two approaches to moving a viewer from an aerial view into a walkable interior tour

Commercial · office campus
  • A Strategy A: Pin based Points of interest marked on showcase, each launching its own tour What problem does it solve?For commercial spaces, there are various entry points and amenities that can’t be represented in one entry point. How does it affect the user?Allows for user initiated discoverability, and provides an overview of what the property contains. Why is it better than the alternative?B shows one interior. Here a pin is a space within one asset — lobby, pavilion, garage — each with its own details.
  • B Strategy B: Simplified Single entry point marked on showcase, launching one interior tour What problem does it solve?Many listings have a single space worth showing. Pins would add choices the property doesn’t have. How does it affect the user?One button to go inside. The aerial orbit establishes context first. Why is it better than the alternative?Simpler scope for single-structure properties, and lower engineering effort to implement.
Residential · community splat
  • A Strategy A: Pin based Points of interest across the community, each launching its own tour What problem does it solve?Home buyers, developers, and builders can evaluate the environmental context of the whole community, then drill into individual structures — the 1:many case a single entry point can’t serve. How does it affect the user?Compares homesites in place, and sees where each one sits relative to the amenities. Why is it better than the alternative?B covers one model home. Here a pin is a parcel, not a room.
  • B Strategy B: Simplified Single entry point on the model home, launching one interior tour What problem does it solve?A single home with one capture has nothing to choose between. How does it affect the user?Orbits the lot to read street, neighbors and setback, then goes inside. Why is it better than the alternative?Simpler scope for single-structure properties, and lower engineering effort to implement.
